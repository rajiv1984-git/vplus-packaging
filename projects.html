Projects

Packaging that works in the real world.

Home  /  Projects
01

Custom Corrugated & Export Packaging

We partner with Rwanda's agricultural exporters and food producers to supply custom-sized, high-strength 3, 5 and 7 ply corrugated cartons. Engineered to withstand stacking and long-distance shipping, our boxes keep fresh produce, tea and goods secure from Kigali to regional markets.

Supplied over 1,000 custom box specifications for farms, bakeries and commercial distributors across Rwanda.
02

Food-Grade & Industrial Plastic Jerricans

Our plastic unit manufactures durable, leak-proof, impact-resistant jerricans and PET/HDPE containers for cooking oil, dairy, beverage, chemical and detergent producers – with custom colours, caps and branding.

Supporting the "Made in Rwanda" drive with steady, high-volume plastic packaging supply since 2019.
03

BOPP Tapes & Flexible Packaging

Transparent, brown and custom-printed BOPP adhesive tapes for carton sealing and logistics, plus laminated and printed flexible packaging for snacks, grains, powders, detergents and confectionery.

Complete packing solutions – carton, tape and wrap – from one local supplier.
04

Branded Restaurant & Café Packaging

We supply pizzerias, fast-food outlets, cafés and bakeries across Kigali with custom-printed pizza boxes, paper cups and sturdy paper bags. Designed to retain heat and stay hygienic, they keep food fresh while showcasing local brands.

Reliable local printing and bulk supply that cuts packaging import delays.
05

Egg Trays & Poultry Supply

Built to protect fragile eggs from farm to market, our shock-absorbing trays are made from recyclable, eco-friendly paper pulp in sizes for small, medium and large eggs.

Supporting Rwanda's growing poultry sector with durable, high-capacity egg tray production.
