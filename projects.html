Home  /  Projects
01

Custom Corrugated & Export Packaging

We partner with Rwanda's leading agricultural exporters and food producers to supply custom-sized, high-strength corrugated cartons. Engineered to withstand stacking and long-distance shipping, our boxes keep fresh produce, tea and goods secure from Kigali to regional markets.

Supplied over 1,000 custom box specifications for local farms, bakeries and commercial distributors across Rwanda.
02

Food-Grade & Industrial Plastic Jerricans

Operating from our Gahanga facility, we manufacture durable, leak-proof plastic jerricans and containers for cooking oil, dairy, beverage and detergent producers. Every batch is molded to strict hygiene and safety standards for reliable retail and bulk storage.

Supporting Rwanda's "Made in Rwanda" drive with steady, high-volume plastic packaging supply since 2019.
03

Branded Restaurant & Bakery Packaging

We supply pizzerias, fast-food outlets, cafes and bakeries across Kigali with custom-printed pizza boxes, burger boxes and sturdy kraft paper bags. Designed to retain heat and resist grease, our food-grade packaging keeps takeaway meals fresh while showcasing local brands.

Reliable local printing and bulk supply helping hospitality businesses reduce packaging import delays.
04

Molded Pulp Egg Trays & Poultry Supply

Built to protect fragile harvests from farm to market, our shock-absorbing molded pulp egg trays prevent breakage during storage and transport. Made from eco-friendly recycled paper fibres, they give poultry farms and distributors a dependable, sustainable packaging solution.

Supporting Rwanda's growing poultry sector with durable, high-capacity egg tray production.
