Company overview

High-quality packaging solutions, made in Rwanda.

V-Plus Packaging Industries Limited is a leading manufacturing company in Rwanda specializing in high-quality packaging. We serve diverse industries with durable, reliable and customized packaging that protects products while enhancing market presentation.

From our head office and factory in Gahanga, Kicukiro, we support "Made in Rwanda" and supply both domestic and regional supply chains. Our contribution to industrial growth earned an official factory visit from the Ministry of Trade and Industry (MINICOM) in 2023.
