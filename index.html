Sustainability

Paper-based packaging for a plastic-free Rwanda.

Rwanda is a global leader in banning single-use plastics. Corrugated board is renewable, recyclable and biodegradable – we are proud to help businesses package responsibly.

🌱

Renewable material

Corrugated board is made from wood fibre – a renewable resource – and recycled paper.

♻️

Fully recyclable

Our cartons can be collected and recycled into new paper and board, keeping fibre in use.

📉

Right-sized design

We design boxes that use only the board you need – less waste, lower cost, lighter loads.

About Vplus

Rwandan manufacturing, regional reach.

Vplus Packaging Ltd is a Kigali-based manufacturer of corrugated paper cartons, operating from the Kigali Special Economic Zone. We help local and regional businesses replace imported packaging with quality cartons made right here in Rwanda – shorter lead times, lower logistics costs and support for Made in Rwanda.

From our factory in Kigali we supply customers across Rwanda and the wider East African region.
