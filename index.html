About us

7 long years of manufacturing excellence, with quality at its best.

Over the past seven years, V-Plus Packaging Industries Ltd has supported Rwanda's local manufacturing sector from its facilities in Ndera and Gahanga. By producing corrugated cartons, food boxes, egg trays and paper bags locally, we have helped Rwandan businesses reduce their reliance on imported packaging.

Alongside strengthening domestic supply chains under the "Made in Rwanda" initiative, V-Plus has created steady jobs and built technical skills for local workers in Kigali. Our contribution to industrial growth earned an official factory visit from the Ministry of Trade and Industry (MINICOM) in 2023.
