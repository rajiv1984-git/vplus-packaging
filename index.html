About Us

7 Long Years Of Manufacturing Excellence
With Quality At Its Best!

V-Plus Packaging Industries Limited is a leading manufacturing company in Rwanda specializing in high-quality packaging solutions. From our head office and factory in Gahanga, Kicukiro, we produce corrugated cartons, plastic jerricans, flexible packaging, BOPP tapes, paper cups, PET/HDPE/PP/PC products, water bottles, domestic products and other specialty packaging items.

We serve diverse industries with durable, reliable and customized packaging – helping businesses protect their products while enhancing market presentation. Our contribution to "Made in Rwanda" earned an official factory visit from the Ministry of Trade and Industry (MINICOM) in 2023.
