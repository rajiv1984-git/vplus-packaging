Company overview

A leading packaging manufacturer in Rwanda.

V-Plus Packaging Industries Limited specializes in high-quality packaging solutions. We produce corrugated cartons, plastic jerricans, flexible packaging, BOPP tapes, BOPP flexible packaging, master batch, core pipes, paper cups, PET/HDPE/PP/PC products, water bottles, domestic products and other specialty packaging items.

Our operations are organized into three manufacturing units:

  • Unit 1 – Corrugation: corrugated cartons, egg trays, paper bags and pizza boxes.
  • Unit 2 – Plastic: plastic jerricans, PET/HDPE/PP/PC products, water bottles and domestic products.
  • Unit 3 – BOPP & Flexible Packaging: BOPP tapes, BOPP flexible packaging, master batch, paper cups and core pipes.
