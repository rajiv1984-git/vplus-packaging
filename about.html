About Us

Smart & Reliable Solutions

Building Long-Term Value For Your Business Is Our Ultimate Goal!

Since 1 October 2014, a leading manufacturing company in Rwanda specializing in high-quality packaging solutions.

We produce a wide range of products including corrugated cartons, plastic jerricans, flexible packaging, BOPP tapes, BOPP flexible packaging, master batch, core pipes, paper cups, PET/HDPE/PP/PC products, water bottles, domestic products and other specialty packaging items.

Our operations are organized into three manufacturing units: Unit 1 – Corrugation, Unit 2 – Plastic and Unit 3 – BOPP & Flexible Packaging. We serve manufacturers, distributors, FMCG companies, agro-processors and chemical producers across domestic and regional supply chains.
